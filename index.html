Developing a Paragraph

Follow along with your teacher: see how a strong paragraph is built, upgrade your grammar, then write your own.

Warm-up

Discuss with a partner. Your teacher will guide you.

  1. When you read a long text in English, how do you know where one idea ends and the next begins?
  2. Look at this “bad paragraph”: “Cities are crowded. I like coffee. Public transport is cheaper. Many people drive.” What is wrong with it?
  3. What small words could connect these ideas?
